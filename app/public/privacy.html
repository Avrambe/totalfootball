Perfect XI Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

What we collect

When you post a result to the leaderboard, we store the display name you type (which can be initials or any name you choose), your game result (such as your Elo, tournament finish, and goal totals), and the squad you drafted. No account, email address, phone number, or device identifier is collected.

How we use it

Leaderboard entries are displayed publicly within the app and on this website so players can compare results. That is the only use.

Third-party services

The app uses Supabase to store leaderboard data. No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.

Data retention

Leaderboard entries are kept indefinitely. Since no personally identifiable information is collected, there is nothing to delete. If you would like a specific leaderboard entry removed, contact us.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The optional display name on the leaderboard is not linked to any identity.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support contact listed on the App Store page or through this website.
